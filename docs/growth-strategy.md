# Ternarie — Growth Strategy

## Overview

Growth is driven by **niche positioning** (hair & beauty), **productized offers** (templates + 48-hour launch), **recurring subscriptions** (hosting/support), and **founder-led marketing** (personal brand → Ternarie). The aim is to acquire customers efficiently and push them toward MRR.

---

## 1. Productized Websites in 48 Hours

**Role**: Core differentiator and conversion offer.

- **Message**: “Launch-ready websites for hair & beauty businesses in 48 hours.”  
- **Execution**: Standardized process (template choice → content intake → setup → go-live) so every launch is repeatable and fast.  
- **Growth impact**: Attracts time-sensitive owners; case studies and testimonials from 48-hour launches become proof for content and sales.

**Action steps**:

- [ ] Document the exact 48-hour process (intake form, content deadline, deploy steps).  
- [ ] Publish a clear “Launch in 48 hours” page on ternarie.com with price and scope.  
- [ ] Collect before/after and testimonials; use in ads, emails, and content.

---

## 2. Niche Down on Beauty

**Principle**: Everything—templates, messaging, SEO, content—targets hair and beauty only.

**Why it works**:

- Higher relevance → better conversion.  
- Clear SEO keywords (e.g. “barber website template,” “hair salon booking system”).  
- Word of mouth within the industry.  
- Easier to create repeatable templates and positioning.

**Action steps**:

- [ ] No generic “small business” messaging; always “hair & beauty” or specific sub-niche.  
- [ ] Build one strong template per sub-niche (barber, hair salon, beauty, nail, lash).  
- [ ] Use niche language in ads, emails, and landing pages.

---

## 3. Industry-Specific Templates as Lead Magnets and Products

**Templates**:

- Barber website template  
- Hair salon website template  
- Beauty salon website template  
- Nail salon website template  
- Lash studio website template  

**Growth use**:

- **SEO**: Pages and content targeting “barber website template,” “hair salon website builder,” etc.  
- **Product**: Sell at $199–$499; some customers self-serve, others upgrade to 48-hour launch.  
- **Proof**: Live demo sites and case studies per niche.

**Action steps**:

- [ ] One landing page per template with clear benefits and CTA (buy template or “Get it done in 48 hours”).  
- [ ] Create content (see [content-strategy.md](content-strategy.md)) that links to these pages.  
- [ ] Consider a “template gallery” or “see it live” section to build trust.

---

## 4. Recurring Hosting and Support as the Main Goal

**Principle**: One-time sales (templates, launch) are valuable, but **MRR is the target**. Every acquisition path should point toward a subscription.

**Tactics**:

- After 48-hour launch, transition customer to Basic or Managed (or Growth).  
- $0 upfront offer drives $199/month × 12 with no lump sum.  
- Onboarding emails explain hosting/support benefits and upsell Managed/Growth where relevant.

**Action steps**:

- [ ] Post-launch sequence in GoHighLevel: thank you → introduce hosting tiers → offer upgrade or $0 upfront for next lead.  
- [ ] Track conversion: launch → first subscription; template buyer → launch or subscription.

---

## 5. Content Marketing and SEO

**Goal**: Attract people already searching for solutions in the niche.

**Target keywords (examples)**:

- “barber website template”  
- “hair salon website builder”  
- “beauty salon booking system”  
- “nail salon website”  
- “lash studio website”  
- “salon website in 48 hours” / “fast salon website”

**Content types**:

- Blog posts and guides (see [content-strategy.md](content-strategy.md)).  
- Template and pricing pages optimized for keywords.  
- Founder-led content on melvinasare.com that links to Ternarie.

**Action steps**:

- [ ] Keyword list and priority (which terms to target first).  
- [ ] Content calendar: 2–4 posts per month on ternarie.com (or melvinasare.com with links to Ternarie).  
- [ ] Internal linking from content to template pages and “Launch in 48 hours.”

---

## 6. Founder-Led Marketing (Personal Brand → SaaS)

**Funnel**: melvinasare.com (authority, traffic) → ternarie.com (product, signup).

**Founder activities**:

- Publish useful content (building websites, marketing for salons, booking systems).  
- Share case studies and “how we built X in 48 hours” stories.  
- Speak to the audience (salon owners, barbers) in content and social.  
- CTA in content and bio: “Get a launch-ready site in 48 hours → Ternarie.”

**Action steps**:

- [ ] Consistent CTAs on melvinasare.com to ternarie.com (sidebar, end of posts, dedicated “Projects” or “Ternarie” page).  
- [ ] One or two founder-led lead magnets (e.g. “Salon website checklist”) that capture email and nurture to Ternarie.  
- [ ] Track traffic and signups from melvinasare.com (UTM or dedicated landing page).

| Channel | Role |
|---------|------|
