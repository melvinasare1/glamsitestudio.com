# Ternarie — Vision & Mission

## Mission

**Ternarie exists to help hair and beauty businesses get online fast, look professional, and book more clients—without the complexity of DIY website builders or the cost of custom agencies.**

We deliver launch-ready websites and booking systems in 48 hours so owners can focus on their craft instead of their tech.

---

## Long-Term Vision

- **Become the default “website + booking” solution** for small hair and beauty businesses in our target markets.
- **Scale through productized delivery**: templates, fixed-scope launch service, and recurring hosting/support—not custom projects.
- **Build a recognizable brand** in the beauty industry: when someone thinks “salon website” or “barber booking,” they think Ternarie.
- **Grow revenue predictably** via subscriptions (hosting, support, software) with one-time and productized offers (templates, launch service) as entry points.

---

## Positioning Statement

**Ternarie is NOT just a template shop.**

We are positioned as:

> **"Launch-ready websites for hair & beauty businesses in 48 hours."**

This means:

- **Speed**: 48-hour launch, not weeks or months.
- **Industry focus**: Built for salons, barbershops, beauty studios, nail and lash businesses—not generic “small business.”
- **Ready to go**: Templates + setup + optional booking and support, so the business can start taking bookings and showing their work immediately.
- **Recurring relationship**: Hosting and support keep us aligned with the customer’s success and create predictable revenue.

---

## What We Are (and Are Not)

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| A productized website + booking service for beauty | A generic website builder or template marketplace |
| Fast (48-hour launch), niche-specific, subscription-led | Slow custom dev shops or one-off freelancers |
| Focused on recurring revenue (hosting, support, software) | A one-time-sale-only template shop |
| A clear path from personal brand (melvinasare.com) to product (ternarie.com) | A side project without a defined funnel |

---

## Success Definition

- **Primary milestone**: $10k in monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
- **Beyond that**: Sustainable growth through more customers on hosting/support plans, more 48-hour launches, and a strong content/SEO engine that feeds the funnel from the founder’s personal brand to Ternarie.

This vision keeps the founder focused on: **niche (beauty), speed (48 hours), and recurring revenue (MRR)**.
