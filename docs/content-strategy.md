# Ternarie — Content Strategy

## Overview

Content and SEO attract hair and beauty businesses who are already looking for websites, booking systems, and templates. The strategy is to target clear keywords, publish consistently, and link content to Ternarie’s offers (templates, 48-hour launch, hosting).

---

## 1. Goals

- **Attract** salon and barber owners searching for solutions (website, booking, templates).  
- **Build authority** in the “salon website” and “beauty business online” space.  
- **Convert** readers into leads (email signup, template interest, launch inquiry) and then into customers (subscription or one-time).  
- **Support SEO** so key pages rank for target keywords and drive long-term traffic.

## 5. Content Standards

- **Audience**: Owner or manager of a hair/beauty business; busy, not technical.  
- **Tone**: Clear, helpful, no jargon. Use “you” and “your salon.”  
- **Length**: 800–1,500+ words for SEO posts; longer for flagship guides.  
- **Structure**: H2/H3 headings, short paragraphs, bullets, one clear CTA (e.g. “See our barber template” or “Get your site in 48 hours”).  
- **Links**: Internal links to template pages, pricing, and launch offer; optional external links to trusted sources.  
- **SEO**: Target one primary keyword per post; use in title, one H2, meta description, and naturally in body.

## 7. Content Calendar (Example)

**Monthly rhythm** (adjust as capacity allows):

- Week 1: One “template” or “builder” post (e.g. barber or hair salon).  
- Week 2: One “booking” or “how-to” post.  
- Week 3: One “cost” or “checklist” or “48 hours” post.  
- Week 4: One case study, update, or repurpose/refresh of an older post.  

**Quarterly**:

- One longer “ultimate guide” (e.g. “Ultimate Guide to Salon Websites and Booking”).  
- Review top 10 keywords and add or update content to target them.
