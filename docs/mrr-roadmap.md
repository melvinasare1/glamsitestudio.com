# Ternarie — MRR Roadmap to $10k

## Primary Milestone

**Goal**: Reach **$10,000 in monthly recurring revenue (MRR)** as fast as possible.

This document breaks down how many customers are needed at each pricing tier and gives example paths to $10k MRR.

---

## 1. Revenue That Counts as MRR

**Counts toward MRR**:

- Basic hosting: $29/month  
- Managed website: $79/month  
- Growth support: $199/month  
- $0 upfront plan: $199/month (12-month minimum)  

**Does not count as MRR** (but supports the business):

- Template sales ($199–$499 one-time)  
- Launch in 48 hours (~$799 one-time)  

One-time revenue funds operations and marketing; the **metric that matters for the milestone is MRR**.

---

## 2. Customer Numbers Needed for $10k MRR

Assuming a **single tier** (all customers on the same plan):

| Plan | Price | Customers needed for $10k MRR |
|------|--------|--------------------------------|
| Basic | $29/mo | 345 customers |
| Managed | $79/mo | **127 customers** |
| Growth | $199/mo | 51 customers |
| $0 upfront | $199/mo | 51 customers |

**Practical target**: **Managed at $79/month** is the best balance—127 customers is achievable with consistent acquisition and retention; 51 on Growth is fewer but harder to close; 345 on Basic is more volume than needed if you can sell Managed.

---

## 3. Mixed Portfolio Examples

Realistically, MRR will come from a **mix** of tiers. Below are example mixes that reach ~$10k MRR.

### Example A: Managed-heavy (recommended)

| Tier | Customers | MRR |
|------|-----------+-----|
| Basic $29 | 20 | $580 |
| Managed $79 | 115 | $9,085 |
| Growth $199 | 2 | $398 |
| **Total** | **137** | **~$10,063** |

Focus: Get to ~115–120 Managed customers; fill the rest with Basic and a few Growth.

### Example B: More Basic + Managed

| Tier | Customers | MRR |
|------|-----------+-----|
| Basic $29 | 50 | $1,450 |
| Managed $79 | 108 | $8,532 |
| **Total** | **158** | **~$9,982** |

Good if you want a lower price point to acquire; then upsell Basic → Managed over time.

### Example C: $0 upfront as accelerator

| Tier | Customers | MRR |
|------|-----------+-----|
| $0 upfront $199 | 30 | $5,970 |
| Managed $79 | 50 | $3,950 |
| Basic $29 | 3 | $87 |
| **Total** | **83** | **~$10,007** |

Fewer total customers because $0 upfront is $199/month; use this offer to reduce friction and lock in 12-month contracts.

## 4. Phased Roadmap (How to Get There)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–2)

- **Goal**: First paying subscribers; process in place.  
- **Actions**: Launch pricing page and Stripe for $29 / $79 / $199. Close first 5–10 hosting customers (from network, content, or outreach). Document 48-hour process and run 2–3 launches.  
- **Target**: ~$500–$1k MRR, 5–15 customers.

### Phase 2: Repeatable acquisition (Months 3–5)

- **Goal**: Consistent new signups from content and funnel.  
- **Actions**: Publish 2–4 SEO posts per month; melvinasare.com → ternarie.com funnel live; GoHighLevel nurture for leads. Offer $0 upfront and 48-hour launch prominently.  
- **Target**: 30–50 total subscribers; ~$2k–$4k MRR.

### Phase 3: Scale to $10k (Months 6–12)

- **Goal**: Reach $10k MRR.  
- **Actions**: Double down on best channels (SEO, founder content, referrals). Optimize conversion (landing pages, email sequences). Consider limited paid (e.g. Facebook/Instagram to salon owners) if CAC is acceptable.  
- **Target**: 127+ subscribers (or mixed portfolio as in examples above); **$10k MRR**.

### Phase 4: Beyond $10k

- **Goal**: Sustain and grow past $10k.  
- **Actions**: Reduce churn (onboarding, support, success check-ins). Expand content and partnerships. Introduce referral or partner program.  
- **Target**: $15k+ MRR; path to $20k.

---

## 5. Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **MRR** | Primary goal; track weekly/monthly. |
| **New subscribers per month** | Acquisition pace. |
| **Churn (canceled subs)** | Retention; fix onboarding and support if high. |
| **Launch → subscription conversion** | % of 48-hour launch customers who take a plan. |
| **Template/lead → launch or subscription** | Funnel efficiency. |
| **CAC (if running ads)** | Cost to acquire a subscriber; keep LTV/CAC healthy. |
| **Average revenue per user (ARPU)** | Mix of $29 vs $79 vs $199; aim to increase ARPU via upsells. |

## 6. Example Growth Model (Numerical)

**Assumptions**:

- Average subscription = $79/month (Managed-heavy).  
- Need **127 customers** for $10k MRR.  
- Monthly churn target: 2–3% (so you need ~3–4 new customers per month just to stay flat at 127; more to grow).

**Rough path**:

- Month 1–3: 5 → 15 → 30 subscribers (~$2.4k MRR by month 3).  
- Month 4–6: 45 → 65 → 90 subscribers (~$5.6k MRR by month 6).  
- Month 7–9: 105 → 118 → 127 subscribers (~$10k MRR by month 9).  

Adjust based on actual signup and churn rates; the important thing is to **track MRR and subscriber count** and prioritize actions that add and retain subscribers.

## 7. Summary

- **Primary milestone**: $10k MRR.  
- **Rough target**: ~127 customers on Managed ($79), or an equivalent mix of Basic / Managed / Growth / $0 upfront.  
- **Roadmap**: Foundation → Repeatable acquisition → Scale to $10k → Beyond.  
- **Metrics**: MRR, new subscribers, churn, launch→subscription conversion, ARPU.  

Use this roadmap to stay focused on **recurring revenue** and to decide how many customers you need at each tier to hit $10k MRR as fast as possible.
