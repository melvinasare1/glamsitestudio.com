# Ternarie — Target Market

## Primary Segments

Ternarie targets **small, local hair and beauty businesses** that need a professional online presence and a simple way to take bookings. Specifically:

| Segment | Description | Typical size |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **Hair salons** | Full-service hair salons, stylists | 1–10 staff |
| **Barbershops** | Men’s grooming, cuts, beard | 1–5 barbers |
| **Beauty salons** | Facials, makeup, skincare | 1–8 staff |
| **Nail salons** | Manicure, pedicure, nail art | 2–15 staff |
| **Lash studios** | Eyelash extensions, brows | 1–5 technicians |
| **Other** | Small local beauty businesses (e.g. waxing, threading) | 1–10 staff |

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

- **Owner or manager** of a hair/beauty business who makes decisions about marketing and operations.
- **Wants more clients** and knows they need a better online presence (website, booking, social proof).
- **Limited time** to build or manage a website; prefers a done-for-you or semi-done solution.
- **Budget**: Can afford $29–$199/month for hosting/support and/or $199–$799 one-time for template or launch.
- **Tech comfort**: Prefers simple tools; may have tried DIY builders (Wix, Squarespace) and found them slow or overwhelming.
- **Location**: Local/regional; often relies on local search and word of mouth.

## Why Hair & Beauty Is a Strong Niche

### 1. **High need for booking**

- Appointments are the core of the business. Online booking reduces no-shows and fills gaps.
- “Hair salon booking system,” “barber booking” are real search terms we can own.

### 2. **Visual, service-based**

- Portfolios, before/after, and branding matter. Templates built for galleries and services fit the industry.

### 3. **Fragmented market**

- Many small independents; few have great websites. Opportunity to be the go-to solution in a defined niche.

### 4. **Recurring need**

- Hosting, updates, and support are ongoing. Fits a subscription model and MRR goals.

### 5. **SEO potential**

- Clear keywords: “barber website template,” “hair salon website builder,” “beauty salon booking system,” “nail salon website,” etc. Content and templates can target these.

### 6. **Word of mouth**

- Stylists and owners talk to each other. One happy customer can lead to referrals in the same niche.

---

## Who We Are Not Targeting (Yet)

- **Enterprise or large chains**: Different needs, longer sales cycles; out of scope for current productized model.
- **Non-beauty businesses**: We stay niche to keep messaging and templates focused.
- **Pure DIYers who want maximum control**: Our value is “done for you” and “launch in 48 hours”; we’re not competing on endless customization.

---

## Summary

- **Ideal customer**: Small hair/beauty business owner who wants a professional site and booking without the hassle.
- **Core problems**: No site, no booking, no time, fear of maintenance, budget constraints.
- **Why this niche**: Booking-centric, visual, fragmented, good for SEO and referrals, and a natural fit for recurring hosting and support.
