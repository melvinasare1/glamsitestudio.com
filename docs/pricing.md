# Ternarie — Pricing

## Overview

Pricing is built around **one-time products** (templates, launch service), **recurring subscriptions** (hosting and support), and one **alternative offer** ($0 upfront + monthly). The aim is to maximize MRR while keeping acquisition clear and simple.

---

## 1. Template Pricing

| Tier | Price | Best for |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Single template** | $199 – $499 | One niche template (e.g. barber, hair salon). Price can vary by template or bundle. |
| **Template + minimal setup** | Higher end of range or add-on | Template + basic config (e.g. placeholder content, booking connected). |

**Guidance**:

- **$199**: Entry-level template, self-serve; customer or a freelancer does the rest.  
- **$299–$499**: Premium template and/or niche-specific (e.g. lash studio, nail salon); can include light setup.  

**Sales channel**: Ternarie site and/or Lemon Squeezy (or similar) for template sales.

---

## 2. Launch in 48 Hours Service

| Item | Price |
|------|--------|
| **Launch in 48 hours** | **~$799** |

**Includes** (fixed scope):

- One Ternarie template  
- Content placement (customer supplies copy and images)  
- Booking system connected and tested  
- Basic SEO (titles, meta)  
- Go-live on Ternarie hosting  
- One round of revisions within 48 hours  

**Upsell**: Customer is encouraged onto a hosting plan (Basic or Managed) after launch so the site stays with Ternarie and generates MRR.

---

## 3. Recurring Subscription Tiers

### Basic Hosting Plan — $29/month

- Hosting for one Ternarie-built site  
- SSL, uptime monitoring  
- Basic updates (security, core)  
- Email support  

**Target**: Customers who want a simple, low-cost way to keep their site live and secure.

---

### Managed Website Plan — $79/month

- Everything in Basic  
- Content/design tweaks (small edits)  
- Plugin and dependency updates  
- Backups  
- Priority support  

**Target**: Owners who want “don’t worry about the site” and light ongoing support.

---

### Growth Support Plan — $199/month

- Everything in Managed  
- Deeper support (more edits, strategy)  
- Marketing automation guidance (e.g. GoHighLevel)  
- Integrations support  
- Closer to a “fractional marketing/tech” relationship  

**Target**: Salons ready to invest in growth and automation; highest LTV and stickiness.

---

## 4. Payment Plan Option: $0 Upfront Website

**Structure**:

- **Upfront cost**: $0  
- **Monthly**: **$199/month**  
- **Minimum**: **12 months**  

**Includes**:

- Website (template-based, set up and launched)  
- Hosting  
- Support (managed-level or as defined)  
- Booking system  

**Why offer it**:

- Removes upfront barrier; good for cash-flow-sensitive businesses.  
- Locks in 12 months of MRR per customer ($199 × 12 = $2,388 LTV).  
- Positions Ternarie as “no big lump sum” and reduces friction for launch.  

**Contract**: Clear 12-month minimum; terms and cancellation policy documented (e.g. what happens after 12 months, early exit).

## 6. Revenue Mix Examples

**Example A — MRR-focused**  
- 127 customers × $79/month (Managed) ≈ **$10,033 MRR**

**Example B — Mixed**  
- 8 launches/month × $799 = $6,392 (one-time)  
- 70 hosting customers × $79 = $5,530 (MRR)  
- Template sales ≈ $1,500 (one-time)  
- **Total ≈ $13k+ monthly** (MRR portion = $5,530; rest one-time/template)

**Example C — $0 upfront**  
- 10 new $0 upfront customers = 10 × $199 = $1,990 added MRR  
- After 12 months, 120 such customers = $23,880 MRR from this offer alone (if all retained)
