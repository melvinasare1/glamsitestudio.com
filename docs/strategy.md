# Ternarie — Business Strategy

## Overview

Ternarie uses a **productized SaaS** model: fixed offers (templates, launch service) plus recurring subscriptions (hosting, support, software). This reduces scope creep, speeds delivery, and builds predictable revenue.

---

## How the Productized SaaS Model Works

### 1. Productized (Fixed Scope, Fixed Price)

- **Templates**: Pre-built designs for barbers, hair salons, beauty salons, nail salons, lash studios. Sold at set price points ($199–$499).
- **Launch in 48 hours**: One defined service—website setup using a template, content placement, basic config—delivered in 48 hours at ~$799. No “custom feature” scope.

**Why it works**: Clear deliverables, no endless revisions, faster delivery, easier to sell and deliver at scale.

### 2. Recurring (SaaS-Style Revenue)

- **Hosting plans**: Basic ($29/mo), Managed ($79/mo), Growth support ($199/mo).
- **Booking software**: Included or bundled with templates and hosting so the business can take bookings from day one.
- **Support & maintenance**: Part of managed and growth tiers so customers stay with us and we earn MRR.

**Why it works**: One-time sales (templates, launch) feed into recurring revenue; the real goal is MRR ($10k target).

### 3. Alternative Entry: $0 Upfront

- **$0 upfront website**: Customer pays $199/month for 12 months minimum. Includes website + hosting + support.
- **Purpose**: Lowers barrier to entry, attracts customers who can’t or won’t pay a big lump sum, and locks in 12 months of MRR per customer.

---

## Strategic Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Niche focus** | Only hair and beauty. Templates and messaging are industry-specific, not generic. |
| **Speed to value** | 48-hour launch. Customer sees a live, bookable site fast. |
| **Recurring first** | Every offer is designed to lead to a subscription (hosting/support). |
| **Founder-led growth** | Personal brand (melvinasare.com) drives authority and traffic into Ternarie (ternarie.com). |
| **Productized delivery** | No custom scope. Templates + setup + defined support tiers. |

---

## Revenue Mix (Target)

- **Recurring**: Hosting and support subscriptions (primary driver of MRR).
- **One-time / productized**: Template sales ($199–$499), 48-hour launch service (~$799).
- **Hybrid**: $0 upfront + $199/month × 12 months = high LTV, predictable MRR.

The strategy is to **maximize MRR** while using one-time and productized offers to acquire and activate customers.

---

## Why This Model Scales

1. **Templates** can be reused; marginal cost per new site is low.
2. **48-hour launch** is a repeatable process, not a new project each time.
3. **Subscriptions** create predictable cash flow and make customer success a priority.
4. **Niche positioning** improves conversion (right message to right audience) and allows for strong SEO (e.g. “barber website template,” “hair salon booking system”).
5. **Personal brand funnel** (melvinasare.com → ternarie.com) brings in warm traffic without depending only on paid ads.

---

## Summary

- **Offer**: Launch-ready websites + booking for hair & beauty in 48 hours.
- **Revenue**: Recurring (hosting, support) + one-time (templates, launch) + payment plan ($0 upfront).
- **Goal**: Reach $10k MRR as fast as possible by productizing delivery and focusing on the beauty niche.
