# Ternarie — Sales Funnel

## Overview

The funnel moves visitors from **awareness** (content, personal brand) to **interest** (templates, 48-hour offer) to **action** (purchase or signup) and finally to **recurring customer** (hosting/support). The main path is **melvinasare.com → ternarie.com → paying customer**.

---

## 1. Funnel Stages

| Stage | What happens | Where |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| **Awareness** | Someone discovers Ternarie or the founder (content, search, social). | melvinasare.com, ternarie.com, search, social |
| **Interest** | They read about templates, 48-hour launch, or booking; maybe sign up for email or download a lead magnet. | Blog, template pages, landing pages |
| **Consideration** | They compare options, look at pricing, maybe book a call or fill a form. | Pricing page, “Launch in 48 hours,” contact/form |
| **Action** | They buy (template, launch) or subscribe (hosting, $0 upfront). | Stripe checkout, signup flow |
| **Retention** | They stay on a plan; we support them and upsell when relevant (e.g. Basic → Managed). | Onboarding, support, email |

---

## 2. Traffic Flow: melvinasare.com → ternarie.com

**Role of melvinasare.com**:

- **Authority**: Founder’s story, expertise, and content build trust.  
- **Traffic**: SEO and social drive visitors to the personal site.  
- **Funnel**: Content and CTAs send them to Ternarie for the product (templates, launch, hosting).

**How traffic moves**:

1. Visitor lands on **melvinasare.com** (blog, about, project page).  
2. They see value (article, guide, or “what I do”).  
3. **CTA** appears: “Get a launch-ready website in 48 hours” / “See Ternarie” / “Templates for salons” → link to **ternarie.com**.  
4. On ternarie.com they hit a **landing page** (template, launch, pricing) or blog.  
5. Next step: **sign up**, **buy**, or **book a call** (depending on offer).

**Action items**:

- [ ] Add a clear “Ternarie” or “Website + booking for salons” section and CTA on melvinasare.com (homepage, sidebar, end of posts).  
- [ ] Use one primary landing page on ternarie.com for personal-brand traffic (e.g. “From Melvin” or “Launch in 48 hours”).  
- [ ] Track traffic and conversions from melvinasare.com (UTM parameters or dedicated link).

## 3. Entry Points on ternarie.com

Visitors can enter the funnel from different angles; each should lead toward a purchase or subscription.

| Entry point | Typical intent | Next step |
|-------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Homepage** | General interest | Navigate to templates, “Launch in 48 hours,” or pricing. |
| **Template page** (e.g. barber, hair salon) | Looking for a template or builder | “Buy template” or “Get it done in 48 hours” or “See pricing.” |
| **Launch in 48 hours** | Wants done-for-you, fast | “Book” or “Get started” → form or Stripe. |
| **Pricing** | Comparing options | Choose plan → Stripe checkout; or $0 upfront. |
| **Blog post** (SEO) | Researched a topic | CTA to template, launch, or pricing. |
| **Lead magnet** | Wants checklist/guide | Give content; follow up in email (GoHighLevel) with offer. |

Ensure every page has **one primary CTA** (e.g. “Start with a template,” “Launch in 48 hours,” “View plans”).

## 4. Conversion Paths (Examples)

### Path A: Content → Template → Launch or Hosting

1. Search “barber website template” → land on ternarie.com barber template page.  
2. Read page; click “Get it done in 48 hours” or “Buy template.”  
3. If 48 hours: pay $799, complete intake, site goes live → then offer hosting (Basic/Managed).  
4. If buy template: checkout; email sequence can offer “we can set it up in 48 hours” or hosting.

### Path B: Personal brand → Launch → Hosting

1. Read post on melvinasare.com about salon websites.  
2. Click CTA “Get your site in 48 hours – Ternarie.”  
3. Land on ternarie.com “Launch in 48 hours” or “From Melvin” page.  
4. Book or pay for launch → after launch, subscribe to Managed (or Basic).

### Path C: $0 upfront (low friction)

1. Land on ternarie.com (any source).  
2. See “$0 upfront – $199/month for 12 months.”  
3. Sign up / apply → qualify if needed → start subscription.  
4. Site built and launched as part of the plan; customer stays for 12+ months.

### Path D: SEO → Blog → Pricing

1. Search “hair salon website builder” or “salon booking system.”  
2. Land on blog or comparison page.  
3. CTA: “See templates” or “View pricing” or “Launch in 48 hours.”  
4. Choose template, launch, or plan → checkout or signup.

## 5. Email / Nurture (GoHighLevel)

**Purpose**: Turn leads (email signups, form fills) into customers.

**Suggested sequences**:

- **Lead magnet signup**: 3–5 emails: value (tips, checklist) → introduce Ternarie → offer template or 48-hour launch or $0 upfront.  
- **Template buyer**: Thank you + delivery; offer “we can set it up in 48 hours” and hosting.  
- **Launch inquiry (no purchase yet)**: 2–3 emails: social proof, what’s included, limited-time or urgency if appropriate.  
- **Post-launch**: Thank you; introduce hosting tiers; encourage upgrade to Managed or Growth.

**Action items**:

- [ ] Create at least one “welcome + offer” sequence in GoHighLevel for new leads.  
- [ ] Add post-purchase sequences (template, launch) that push toward hosting.  
- [ ] Sync form submissions (ternarie.com, melvinasare.com) to GoHighLevel.
