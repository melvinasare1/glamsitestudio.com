# Ternarie — Product Offers

## Overview

Ternarie’s product structure is built around **templates**, **launch service**, **booking software**, **hosting**, and **support**. Each offer is designed to be productized (clear scope) and to lead into recurring revenue where possible.

---

## 1. Website Templates

**What it is**: Pre-designed, industry-specific website templates for hair and beauty businesses. Customers (or Ternarie during the launch service) add content, branding, and connect booking.

**Templates by niche**:

- Barber website template  
- Hair salon website template  
- Beauty salon website template  
- Nail salon website template  
- Lash studio website template  

**Includes (typical)**:

- Homepage with hero, services, gallery, testimonials  
- Services/pricing pages  
- About/team  
- Contact  
- Integration points for booking system  
- Mobile-responsive, fast-loading design  

**Positioning**: Not a generic “business template”—built for salons and barbers so the site feels on-brand from day one.

**Revenue**: One-time purchase ($199–$499). Can be sold via Ternarie site or a platform like Lemon Squeezy.

---

## 2. Launch in 48 Hours Service

**What it is**: A productized setup service. Customer chooses a template; Ternarie configures it with their content (text, images, logo), connects booking, and delivers a live site in 48 hours.

**Scope (fixed)**:

- One template from the Ternarie library  
- Content placement (customer provides copy and images; we place and optimize)  
- Basic SEO (titles, meta, structure)  
- Booking system connected and tested  
- Go-live on Ternarie hosting (or customer’s domain)  
- One round of revisions within the 48-hour window  

**Out of scope**: Custom design, custom features, multiple rounds of redesign, ongoing content creation.

**Price**: ~$799 (see [pricing.md](pricing.md) for full structure).

**Purpose**: High-value one-time offer that demonstrates speed and quality; natural upsell to hosting and support plans.

---

## 3. Booking System Software

**What it is**: Software that lets clients book appointments online 24/7. Integrated with the website so “Book now” leads to a seamless experience.

**Typical features**:

- Service and duration  
- Staff/technician selection (if applicable)  
- Calendar availability  
- Notifications (confirmations, reminders)  
- Optional: deposits, packages, memberships  

**Delivery**: Included or bundled with templates and hosting so every launched site can take bookings from day one.

**Positioning**: Part of the “website + booking” package—not sold as a standalone product initially. Focus is on the full solution (site + booking) for hair and beauty.

---

## 4. Website Hosting

**What it is**: Hosting for Ternarie-built (template-based) sites. Ensures speed, security, and uptime so the business doesn’t have to manage servers.

**Tiers**: Basic ($29/mo), Managed ($79/mo). See [pricing.md](pricing.md) for details.

**Includes (by tier)**:

- **Basic**: Hosting, SSL, basic updates, uptime monitoring.  
- **Managed**: Everything in Basic + content/design tweaks, plugin updates, backup, priority support.  

**Purpose**: Recurring revenue and an ongoing relationship; customers stay on Ternarie instead of moving to another host.

---

## 5. Maintenance and Support

**What it is**: Ongoing help with updates, small changes, and issues so the business owner doesn’t have to touch the site.

**Delivery**:

- **Managed plan ($79/mo)**: Includes maintenance and light support.  
- **Growth support plan ($199/mo)**: Deeper support—more edits, strategy, marketing automation guidance, integrations (e.g. GoHighLevel).  

**Purpose**: Reduces churn, increases LTV, and differentiates from “just hosting.” Support is a key reason to stay subscribed.

---

## 6. Marketing Automation Integrations

**What it is**: Connections between the website/booking system and tools like GoHighLevel (CRM, email, SMS, funnels) so salons can run simple campaigns, reminders, and follow-ups.

**Positioning**: Emphasized in the **Growth support** tier ($199/mo). We help set up or advise on:

- Email/SMS sequences  
- Review requests  
- Rebooking reminders  
- Simple funnels (e.g. lead capture → booking)  

**Purpose**: Higher-value tier and stickier customers; positions Ternarie as a growth partner, not just a website vendor.

---

## Product Flow (How Offers Work Together)

```
Template (one-time) or $0 upfront
         ↓
Launch in 48 hours (one-time) or included in $0 upfront
         ↓
Hosting plan (recurring): Basic or Managed
         ↓
Optional: Growth support (recurring) — more support + marketing automation
```

**Alternative path**: Customer pays $0 upfront and $199/month × 12 months → website + hosting + support included; no separate template or launch fee.
